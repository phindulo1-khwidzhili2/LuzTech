Questions

Is it worth repairing my old laptop?

Often, yes. A faster drive and more memory can make an older laptop feel new for a fraction of the cost of replacing it. If it isn't worth fixing, we'll tell you.

Will I lose my files?

Where the drive can still be read, we back up your files before any work that could put them at risk.

Can you help me choose a new computer?

Yes. Tell us what you use it for and your budget, and we'll recommend something that fits, then set it up for you.
