Many computer problems can be fixed over the internet in minutes, without waiting for a visit. You watch everything we do on your screen.

Request remote help

Fixed in four easy steps

It takes about two minutes to get connected.

  1. 1

    Message us first

    Send us a WhatsApp saying you need remote help and what's going wrong.

  2. 2

    Download AnyDesk

    It's free and safe. On Windows it runs straight from the download, with nothing to install.

  3. 3

    Send us your address

    Open AnyDesk and send us the number shown as your address. Click Accept when we connect.

  4. 4

    Watch while we fix it

    You see everything we do. When we're done, close AnyDesk and the connection ends.
