Bringing Light to Technology

Luztech takes its name from Kganya, meaning “light”, and Luz, meaning light in Spanish and Portuguese. Inspired by my son, Kganya, the name carries a personal meaning and represents the vision behind the business.

Luztech was founded to illuminate the path forward in an increasingly complex digital world.

With over 9 years of hands-on IT experience in mining and corporate environments, we bring practical industry knowledge, technical expertise and a solutions-driven approach to every project.

Our mission

To demystify technology and engineer reliable, innovative and seamless solutions that work for you, not against you.
